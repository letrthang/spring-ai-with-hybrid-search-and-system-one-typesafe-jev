# Green Pond Farm — Aerator manual

## Aerator AER-01 to AER-06
The farm has six paddle-wheel aerators, AER-01 to AER-06, two per pond. Each one is 2 HP and moves about
2 kg of oxygen per hour into the water. AER-01 and AER-02 are on Pond 1, AER-03 and AER-04 on Pond 2, AER-05 and
AER-06 on Pond 3.

## Reset after a trip (error E-17)
If an aerator stops and its panel shows E-17, the motor overload relay has tripped. Switch the panel off, wait
2 minutes, then hold the green RESET button for 5 seconds and switch on again. If E-17 comes back within an hour,
check the paddles for fishing net or plastic, and call the electrician.

## Running schedule
Run both aerators of a pond from 10 pm to 7 am every night. In hot or cloudy weather, also run one aerator from
1 pm to 4 pm. Never switch all six on at the same second: start them one minute apart so the generator is not
overloaded.

## Maintenance
Every Monday, grease the gearbox of each aerator (two pumps of the grease gun) and check the float is level.
Every three months, change the gearbox oil (1.5 L, SAE 90).
