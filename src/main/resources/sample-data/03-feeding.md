# Green Pond Farm — Feeding guide

## Daily ration
Feed 3 % of the fish body weight per day for fingerlings under 50 g, 2 % for fish of 50 to 300 g, and 1.5 % for
fish above 300 g. Split the ration into two meals, at 8 am and 4 pm.

## When not to feed
Do not feed when dissolved oxygen is below 3 mg/L, when the water is colder than 20 °C, or in the hour after a
treatment. Fish that do not eat leave feed in the pond, which rots and uses up even more oxygen.

## Checking appetite
Throw a small handful of feed first. If the fish take it within 5 minutes, give the rest of the meal. If feed is
still floating after 15 minutes, stop and write it in the logbook.

## Feed storage
Keep feed bags off the floor on pallets, in a dry room, and use them within 3 months of the production date. Mouldy
feed must be thrown away: it can poison the fish.
