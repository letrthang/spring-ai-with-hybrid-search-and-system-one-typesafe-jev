# Green Pond Farm — Water quality guide

## Dissolved oxygen (DO)
Dissolved oxygen is the most important water reading on the farm. Tilapia and catfish need at least 5 mg/L.
Between 3 and 5 mg/L the fish eat less and grow slowly. Below 3 mg/L they are in danger and below 2 mg/L fish
start to die within hours. Oxygen is lowest just before sunrise, because algae use oxygen during the night.

## Signs of low oxygen
When oxygen is too low, fish swim at the surface and gulp air with their mouths open, as if gasping for breath.
They gather near the inlet or the aerators, stop eating, and move slowly. If you see fish breathing at the top of
the water in the early morning, switch on every aerator at once and measure DO.

## pH
Keep pH between 6.5 and 8.5. A pH above 9 in the afternoon usually means an algae bloom. A pH below 6 after heavy
rain needs agricultural lime: 10 kg per 1,000 m² of pond surface, spread along the banks.

## Water temperature
Tilapia grow best at 26 to 30 °C. Above 32 °C they need more oxygen and eat less. Below 20 °C they stop eating.
In hot weather, add 10 % fresh water in the morning and run the aerators longer.

## Ammonia
Total ammonia should stay below 0.5 mg/L. High ammonia comes from overfeeding and dead fish. Stop feeding for one
day, remove dead fish and exchange 20 % of the water.
