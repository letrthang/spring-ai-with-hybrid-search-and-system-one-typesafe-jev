# Green Pond Farm — Sensor alarms

## Alarm codes
The pond sensors send these alarm codes to the farm phone:
- `DO-LOW`: dissolved oxygen below 4 mg/L for 10 minutes.
- `DO-CRIT`: dissolved oxygen below 2.5 mg/L.
- `PH-HIGH` / `PH-LOW`: pH outside 6.5 – 8.5.
- `TEMP-HIGH`: water warmer than 32 °C.
- `SENSOR-OFFLINE`: no reading for 30 minutes.

## What to do on DO-CRIT
DO-CRIT is an emergency. Switch on every aerator of that pond, stop feeding, and if you have one, start the
backup pump to bring in fresh water. Check the pond again after 30 minutes. Call the farm manager if DO is still
below 3 mg/L after one hour.

## Sensor cleaning
Clean the DO sensor membrane every week with a soft cloth and clean water. A dirty membrane reads too low and causes
false DO-LOW alarms. Calibrate the sensor in air once a month.
