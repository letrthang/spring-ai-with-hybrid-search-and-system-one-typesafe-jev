# Physics basics — forces and motion

## Newton's three laws
First law: an object keeps moving at the same speed in a straight line unless a force acts on it. Second law: force
equals mass times acceleration, F = m · a. Third law: when one object pushes on another, the other pushes back with
an equal force in the opposite direction.

## Momentum
Momentum is mass times velocity, p = m · v. In a collision with no outside force, the total momentum before equals
the total momentum after. That is why a heavy truck is much harder to stop than a bicycle at the same speed.

## Energy and work
Work is force times distance, W = F · d, measured in joules. Kinetic energy is the energy of motion, ½ m v²; potential
energy is stored energy, for example m g h for an object lifted to height h. Energy is never created or destroyed,
only changed from one form into another.
